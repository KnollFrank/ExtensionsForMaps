Extensions for Maps is a free open-source utility that adds automatic route optimization and extension to Google Maps.

Features:

- Automatic detection of routes in Google Maps
- Expands routes beyond the standard Google Maps stop limit
- Optimizes stop order based on road network (OpenRouteService) or straight-line distance
- Displays floating controls directly over Google Maps
- 100% open source, privacy-friendly, and ad-free

Extensions for Maps is an independent open-source utility for Android and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC or Google Maps.
