Route Sorter for Maps is a free open-source utility that automatically sorts routes from Google Maps to find the shortest path.

Features:

- Sorts the order of route stops to minimize total travel distance
- Sorts stop order based on road network (OpenRouteService) or straight-line distance
- 100% open source, privacy-friendly, and ad-free

Route Sorter for Maps is an independent open-source utility for Android and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC or Google Maps.
